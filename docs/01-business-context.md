# 1. Business context and scope

## Problem

A typical multi-donor NGO runs several grants at the same time. Each donor has its own rules:

- **Eligible costs** — what the money may be spent on (e.g. one donor funds equipment, another does not).
- **Grant period** — costs outside the start/end date are not reimbursed.
- **Budget lines** — spending is reported per line, and overspending a line needs donor approval.
- **Procurement thresholds** — above certain amounts, several quotes or an open tender are mandatory.

When these rules live in spreadsheets and email approvals, the usual results are ineligible costs found at audit,
overspent budget lines, and donor reports that don't agree with the ledger.

## Goal of the prototype

Show how the rules can be enforced **at the moment of the purchase request**, not at audit time, and how the
accounting entries can be generated automatically and stay reconcilable to the donor report.

## In scope

| Area | What is covered |
|---|---|
| Grants and budgets | Grants with period, donor, eligible cost categories; budget lines with amounts |
| Purchase request | Entry with validation: eligibility, period, funds available, procurement rule |
| Approval | Amount-based chain, Grant Manager added when a line is almost used up, reject with reason |
| Purchase-to-pay | Receipt (with tolerance), invoice matching, payment |
| Budget control | Pending, committed (encumbrance) and actual amounts per budget line |
| Accounting | Journals generated by rules; account built from segments Entity-Account-Fund-BudgetLine-CostCentre |
| Reporting | Donor view: budget vs actual vs committed; SQL control and reconciliation queries |

## Out of scope (next iterations)

Multi-currency and exchange-rate differences, partial receipts, indirect cost recovery (overhead %), cost
re-allocation between grants, payroll cost allocation, supplier master data, attachments, user authentication.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Requester / Budget Holder | Raises the request, first approval for their budget |
| Grant Manager | Owns the donor relationship; approves when a line is close to its limit |
| Finance Manager | Financial approval from 1,000; owns month-end and donor reporting |
| Country Director | Final approval from 10,000 |
